HIGH LEVEL

MARKET SNAPSHOT

Market is hot and crowded. Lucid Dreams' 1.1M monthly users dwarfs uhoney's 10.6K — a 100x gap where uhoney was 21x us nine weeks ago. That means either lucid dreams' user count inflated or the market consolidated downward in early sept 2026. The new entrants (AERA, Hot Chat, Secret Stars, Softie) add noise but no mega-player emerged since 09-10. No exits, no funding announcements, no regulatory changes announced in september 2026.

MONETIZATION LAYERS ARE LOCKED

Affiliate programs top out at 10-20% to avoid spam. Star value is frozen at $0.013-0.015. Ad networks pay $0.012-0.02 CPM or $2-6 per rewarded unit depending on format. Onlychat AI proves there's a 900-creator market where agencies pay 10% + $70/month to automate their telegram monetization, pulling $2M+/month revenue — but that's a separate business (creator-owned accounts, not clone operator scale).

ATTRIBUTION IS STILL BROKEN FOR BUSINESS CONNECTIONS

Start_param and affiliate deep links exist in the API and work fine for mini apps, but mini apps need a bot front door. Our clones are business connections with no bot, no mini app, no /start. That structural fact hasn't changed and explains why users.source is still null on all 78k+ fans.

AGE VERIFICATION EXISTS BUT DOES NOT SHIP

Telegram ships an official age-verification mini app with on-device face scan and web_app_verify_age event, but competitors do not use it. All 18+ mini apps gate with a self-tap and call themselves verified. Telegram's own policy does not enforce the check during signup, so it's advisory only. This is unchanged from 09-10.

DIRECTORIES OFFER FREE LISTINGS, PAID FEATURED PLACEMENTS EXIST BUT ARE OPAQUE

FindMini (227 AI character apps), MiniTelegram, and Tapps.Center all list apps free. Featured trending and newsletter ads are available on FindMini but pricing is not published online — it's a sales ask. Ronan's prior figure of "1000+ high-intent users per week" for featured placement hasn't been updated or verified in 2026 sources.

ONLYCHAT AI IS NOT A DIRECT COMPETITOR

It's a separate platform for creators (not clone operators) who connect their own telegram business accounts, and Onlychat adds the AI agent and takes 10% + $20-70/month. They've pulled $2M+/month with 900 creators, which proves the creator-monetization market exists. But it runs parallel to our funnel, not through it.
