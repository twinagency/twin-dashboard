LOW LEVEL

WHAT IT IS
- Separate mini app, separate bot, separate brand. Not a twin-bot feature.
- Anyone makes an account, uploads content, sets a price, sells it.
- We are a bridge only: list content, route payment to the creator's own wallet
  or link, deliver content once payment is confirmed. We never hold money.
- Direct competitor to Telescope (1.6M MAU, #5 top-grossing Telegram mini app,
  see TELESCOPE-RESEARCH-2026-09-28.txt).

3 PAGES, NOT MORE
1. EXPLORE — infinite scroll feed, mixed photos/videos, mixed free/paid, all
   creators together. TikTok-style single-column scroll.
2. TRENDING — leaderboard of creators, ranked by sales/activity. Telescope's
   discovery-layer model.
3. ACCOUNT — the fan's own tab: purchases, saved items, and (if they're also
   a creator) their own upload/pricing dashboard. One account type, not two
   separate apps — same login can browse and sell.

PAYMENTS — THE BRIDGE MODEL (DECIDED)
- Every creator (ours and external, no difference) provides ONE thing at
  signup: a TON wallet address, or a Stars bot link if they'd rather start
  there. No special treatment for our own creators (Alanna etc.) — same form,
  same fields, same flow.
- Fan flow: connect TON wallet once (TON Connect, Telegram's native wallet
  picker) -> browse -> tap buy -> TON Connect sends payment directly to the
  creator's wallet, tagged with a comment/memo identifying the purchase.
- We never touch the money. No payout job, no ledger, no KYC on our side.
- Stars path (simpler, ship first): creator gives a link (their own bot or
  Twin's existing bot). Fan taps buy, link opens, they pay there. We don't
  create invoices or call webhooks — just open the link.

THE ONE HARD TECHNICAL PART — PAYMENT CONFIRMATION (verified against TON docs)
- TON Connect's sendTransaction call does NOT confirm payment. It resolves as
  soon as the wallet signs and broadcasts — before the transaction reaches
  finality. TON's own guidelines say explicitly: "You should never use
  external message tracking for payment processing purposes."
- Correct flow: tag every transaction with a comment/memo (order id) using
  the payload field. Our backend polls the creator's wallet address via a TON
  API (TonAPI or toncenter), matches the incoming transaction by comment +
  amount, and only delivers content once that transaction is confirmed
  on-chain — not when the frontend promise resolves.
- Typical finality: ~1 second per transaction, but poll with a few seconds of
  margin. This is a genuinely new piece of backend code (a small poller/
  matcher service), not something TON Connect gives us for free.
- Get this wrong and a fan could screenshot "payment sent" while the transfer
  never lands, and we'd hand over content for nothing. This is the one place
  actual engineering care matters, not vibes.

HIGH LEVEL

A lean, 3-page Telegram mini app (Explore, Trending, Account) where anyone
signs up, lists content, and gets paid directly to their own TON wallet or
via their own Stars link. We are purely a bridge: host the listing, route the
buy click, and deliver content once payment is independently confirmed. No
payout system, no KYC, no money held on our side, same rules for our own
creators as for anyone else who signs up. The one real engineering piece is
verifying TON payments correctly — the wallet SDK's "sent" signal is not the
same as "confirmed," and skipping that check is the one way this could
actually lose money. Ship Stars-only first (it's just an outbound link),
add TON once the confirmation poller is built and tested.
