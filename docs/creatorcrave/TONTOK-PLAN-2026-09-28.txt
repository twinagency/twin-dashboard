TONTOK — PLAN 2026-09-28
Open PPV marketplace inside Telegram. Anyone signs up, uploads, sells, we take a cut.

LOW LEVEL

WHAT IT IS
- A separate mini app, separate bot, separate brand ("TonTok"). Not a twin-bot feature.
- Anyone makes an account, uploads content, sets prices, sells it. Their personal
  Telegram promotes their TonTok page, but the two stay separate accounts.
- Direct competitor to Telescope (1.6M MAU, #5 top-grossing Telegram mini app,
  see TELESCOPE-RESEARCH-2026-09-28.txt).

PAYMENTS — DECIDED
- Launch on Telegram Stars, same checkout code already built and proven in
  twin-dashboard (catalogue-checkout.ts, catalogue-api.ts, the Catalogue.tsx
  mini app). Reuse it, don't rewrite it.
- Stars can't split one payment to two accounts. We hold the balance, pay
  creators out on a schedule (weekly/monthly), take our % before payout. Same
  21-day-hold constraint we already live with on Twin.
- TON added later as a second option: pure crypto-in, no fiat, no KYC on our
  side (see PAYMENTS-TONPAY-RESEARCH files). Fan connects a TON wallet via TON
  Connect, pays with TON Pay, it lands straight in the creator's own wallet,
  we never touch it. Real, but TON Pay itself has no card/Apple Pay path yet
  — that requires a third-party on-ramp (Mercuryo is the easiest, it's already
  the on-ramp baked into Tonkeeper) and doesn't remove KYC, it just moves whose
  problem it is. Don't block launch on this.

WHY NOT CARD-TO-TON OURSELVES
- Checked twice. TON's own SDK is blockchain-only, no Apple Pay/card method.
- Any fiat-touching payment always passes through a KYC'd party somewhere:
  Apple/Google IAP for Stars, or a licensed on-ramp (Mercuryo/MoonPay/Transak)
  for direct TON. No documented way for us to sit in that chain without
  either being the licensed party or paying one to be.
- Only path with zero KYC on our side: fan already holds TON, pays wallet to
  wallet. That's the TON Pay use case, not a card replacement.

WHAT TO REUSE FROM TWIN (BUILD NOTHING TWICE)
- Mini app shell, item grid, bundle cards, blur/unlock UI: app/catalogue/[slug]
- Checkout, ownership, delivery, refunds: lib/catalogue-checkout.ts,
  lib/catalogue-api.ts
- Payment flow just fixed today: pay -> close to chat -> photo shows clear on
  return. Same pattern applies here, chat = TonTok's own DM/bot thread instead
  of a business connection.

WHAT'S NEW, NOT A TWIN FEATURE
1. Self-serve creator signup (name, TON wallet address for payout, no wallet
   needed yet if launching Stars-only)
2. Multi-tenant storefronts inside one app (Telescope's card/profile model)
3. Our cut + payout scheduling logic (doesn't exist anywhere in Twin, we've
   only ever paid ourselves)
4. Content moderation / who's allowed to sign up — we're still the platform
   host even though creators handle their own payment side. This is the one
   piece that doesn't disappear just because payment is decentralized.
5. Anonymous ops structure (LLC/shell, no personal names), same as Telescope
   and every other player in this space — do this before going public/listed
   in any directory.

HIGH LEVEL

TonTok is a new product, not a Twin feature: an open marketplace where
anyone signs up, uploads and sells, aimed at the same space Telescope already
proves is worth $1.6M MAU/top-5-grossing. We already own the hardest part,
a working Stars checkout, mini app and delivery pipeline, so the fastest path
is Stars-first using that exact code, with TON added later as a second,
lower-fee option once TON Pay itself supports it properly. The one thing that
doesn't get outsourced by "creators handle their own payments" is who we let
sign up and what they're allowed to post — that responsibility stays ours
either way, and needs a decision before this goes anywhere near a public
directory listing.
