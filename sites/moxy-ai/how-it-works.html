What is Moxy AI?

Moxy AI helps creators build a personalised AI that chats with fans inside Telegram. It is shaped by your profile, personality and preferences, giving fans another way to connect with your creator identity.

What do I need to get started?

Start by creating an account. You’ll share details about your personality, texting style, example replies and boundaries, then provide the approved content you want to use. The team will guide you through getting your setup ready.

How long does setup take?

Timing depends on how complete your profile and content are, and what your setup needs. We’ll confirm the next steps and launch timing with you during onboarding.

How can I earn with Moxy AI?

Options include paid messages, exclusive content, tips and gifts, with subscription options available to discuss during setup. The team will help you understand which options apply to your creator account.

What does it cost, and how do payouts work?

Contact us for the current creator or agency terms. We’ll explain the revenue split, any applicable fees, payment methods and payout schedule before you decide to launch.

Do fans need a separate app?

The fan conversation happens inside Telegram. You share your AI’s Telegram link once your setup is ready.

Will fans know they’re chatting with AI?

Yes. Your AI is identified as an AI version of you, so fans understand who they’re speaking with.

Can I add my voice?

Voice onboarding is temporarily paused while we prepare to bring it back. You can start with your text personality now; voice recording is not required to begin.

Can an agency bring multiple creators?

Yes. Get in touch about your roster so we can discuss creator setup, team workflows and the arrangement that fits your agency.
