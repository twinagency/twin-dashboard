How it’s calculated

Assumes 3% of social followers or 15% of platform subscribers join your AI each month. We use the larger estimate, without adding the two audiences together. Then 10% purchase, spending US$50 per buyer that month.

These are optimistic planning assumptions selected for this calculator, not measured averages. Actual revenue may be lower or zero. Amounts are in USD, before fees and revenue splits.

Your current income is used only for comparison. Results depend on promotion, engagement and spending; this models new fans joining that month.
